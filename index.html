      Setting up a room
      
      
        
      
        The steps (in a headset: the menu, Circles, Room setup)
        
          
          
        1. Before: each Quest scans this room once (Settings, Physical space, Space setup, with Physical space features on). Every headset on the same Quest Browser version, the same bookmarked address and the same Wi-Fi. Two fixed spots, A and B, about 2 m apart: two corners of a table that stays put, or two tape crosses on the floor with an arrow from A to B.
          2. 
          
        2. Line up, one headset at a time (each headset's menu opens at it by itself as it enters AR): the first headset picks the two spots and presses Calibrate: the bright ring at the controller's tip on spot A, the trigger, then spot B. That sets the room. Each of the others calibrates on the same spots, or goes straight to the check when it is lined up by them already. A VR headset elsewhere skips this.
          3. 
          
        3. Check: Check at A, then the ring on spot A itself and the trigger. Up to 10 cm: lined up (the menu closes). 10 to 25 cm: fine for walking about. Over 25 cm: not lined up, back to Line up to calibrate again. With someone, Check here on both headsets within 20 s, both tips on one spot (the only check while the room has no spots). Everyone's name tag should float just above the real person's head.
          4. 
          
        4. Not lined up: Re-align on that headset, then check again. Still out: Calibrate on the two spots, on every headset (spot A, then spot B). Still out: check each headset's room scan and browser version, then Fresh shared space (everyone leaves AR; one headset first, the others after Room ready).
