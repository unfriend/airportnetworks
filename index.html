      Setting up a room
      
      
        
      
        The steps (in a headset: the menu, Circles, Room setup)
        
          
          
        1. Before: each Quest scans this room once (Settings, Physical space, Space setup, with Physical space features on). Every headset on the same Quest Browser version, the same bookmarked address and the same Wi-Fi. Two tape crosses on the floor, A and B, about 2 m apart (1 m at least), with an arrow from A to B: they set the room and its floor.
          2. 
          
        2. Line up, one headset at a time (each headset's menu opens at it by itself as it enters AR): the first headset presses Calibrate: the controller pointing down, the bright ring at its tip on spot A on the floor, the trigger, then spot B. That sets the room and its floor, whatever each headset's own floor says. Spots at different heights, or spot A well above the floor (a table), are refused: touch them again on the floor. Each of the others calibrates on the same spots; one lined up by them already is asked by eye instead (below). A VR headset elsewhere skips this.
          3. 
          
        3. Check: Check at A, then the ring on spot A itself, on the floor, and the trigger (the result says how much of it is in height when that is 2 cm or more: a headset's floor that differs). Up to 10 cm: lined up (the menu closes). 10 to 25 cm: fine for walking about. Over 25 cm: not lined up, back to Line up to calibrate again. With someone, Check here on both headsets within 20 s, both tips on one spot (the only check while the room has no spots). Everyone's name tag should float just above the real person's head. By eye: at the start of each session, after waking and whenever the room may have moved in a headset, it shows its two orange crosses where it puts the spots and asks Are both crosses on your spots? Yes counts as a check; No, calibrate again opens Line up.
          4. 
          
        4. Not lined up: Re-align on that headset, then check again. Still out: Calibrate on the two spots, on every headset (spot A, then spot B). Still out: check each headset's room scan and browser version, then Fresh shared space (everyone leaves AR; one headset first, the others after Room ready).
          5. 
        
        
      
        
      
        
        
        The PC's trackers
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        Lining up by hand: lay a tracker flat on spot A (the tape cross on the floor), pick it here, press Set spot A and leave it still for a second; then the same on spot B. It holds while the base stations stay where they are and SteamVR's room setup is not run again. Move a tracker to see which row is which, and give it a name.
