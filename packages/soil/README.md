# Soil microbes: Holding on to nitrogen (`#soil`)

Less than half of the fertiliser a farmer spreads ends up in the crop. In a field trial in Japan, a wheat with a trait from a wild grass stays greener and leaks less nitrate, while the soil's microbes follow the fertiliser far more than the wheat.

One of the app's own stories, a story package since it moved here (docs/packages-migration.md). Made by the app's authors;
its sources, its build and its notes are in `src/` (`src/README.md`, `src/SPEC.md` where there is one): `node packages/soil/src/build.mjs`
writes `main.js`, then `node tools/packages/scan.mjs`. Its data and the permission to show it: `docs/data-and-permissions.md`.
