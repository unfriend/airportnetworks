# Disease endotypes: Endotypes (`#endo`)

A group of rare diseases of the junction between nerve and muscle, 35 genes and 12 endotypes: how a random walk and a tree that splits only when the data say so find them.

One of the app's own stories, a story package since it moved here (docs/packages-migration.md). Made by the app's authors;
its sources, its build and its notes are in `src/` (`src/README.md`, `src/SPEC.md` where there is one): `node packages/endo/src/build.mjs`
writes `main.js`, then `node tools/packages/scan.mjs`. Its data and the permission to show it: `docs/data-and-permissions.md`.
