# The switch that sticks: CDK5 and brain diseases (`#cdk5`)

CDK5 is a switch in neurons that two partners turn on. Among 16,376 human proteins, follow it from healthy signalling to
the cut that leaves it stuck on, as in Alzheimer's disease, and into a landscape of diseases where it sits with disorders
of the developing brain.

Written by Christiane Hütter (the Menche lab) on a fork of the app at v135, after the lab's public repository
**CDK5_data** (github.com/menchelab/CDK5_data: its CDK5_analytics VR project, six scenes on the whole human interactome
made with cartoGRAPHs), and brought into the app as a story package at v189 (on the Experiments set of cards until it
has been tried on a headset). Its sources, its build and its notes are in `src/` (`src/README.md`, `src/SPEC.md`):
`node packages/cdk5/src/build.mjs` writes `main.js`, then `node tools/packages/scan.mjs`. Narrated with a synthetic voice
(`audio/narration.mp3`, 8:27). Its data and the permission to show it: `docs/data-and-permissions.md`; open questions
for the authors: `docs/questions-for-authors.md` (C1 to C10).
