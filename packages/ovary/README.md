# Ovarian aging: The aging ovary (`#ovary`)

Sections of 255 ovaries from donors aged 20 to 70, read by deep learning: follicles run out, scars pile up and blood vessels grow fewer. Two methods, one picture of aging.

One of the app's own stories, a story package since it moved here (docs/packages-migration.md). Made by the app's authors;
its sources, its build and its notes are in `src/` (`src/README.md`, `src/SPEC.md` where there is one): `node packages/ovary/src/build.mjs`
writes `main.js`, then `node tools/packages/scan.mjs`. Its data and the permission to show it: `docs/data-and-permissions.md`.
