Sound board

Every sound effect the app's shell can play (sfx.js), made from Web Audio nodes as the app makes them. In the app they are off until turned on (Sound check on the Stories screen, or the menu on the left wrist in a headset), and they sit well under the narration. Press Start sound first.

Interface and room sounds

Left and Right play it panned, as page buttons are by where they sit on the screen. In a headset the room sounds come from where they happen.

Backgrounds

One quiet loop for the story on show, faded in once it is built and out when it goes. Every story without one of its own gets the calm tone, its pitch, brightness and pace from its key.

3D demo

A small sound circling your head at ear height, 1.5 m away, through the same HRTF panner a headset uses (the listener at your head). Use headphones: on speakers it is only left and right.

Ducking demo

A background and a few clicks; after two seconds seven seconds of the airports' narration play, and everything else dips 9 dB under the voice (down in about 0.4 s, back in about 1.5 s). Without the recording a stand-in voice plays.

Voice limit

At most 16 sounds at once: the one quietest now makes room for the next.
