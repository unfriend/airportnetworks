Sound board

Every sound effect the app's shell can play (sfx.js), made from Web Audio nodes as the app makes them: the shell's own, and the stories' (phase 2). In the app they are on unless turned off (Sound check on the Stories screen, or the menu on the left wrist in a headset; the Sound button silences them with the narration), and they sit well under the narration. Press Start sound first.

not started

Interface and room sounds

Left and Right play it panned, as page buttons are by where they sit on the screen. In a headset the room sounds come from where they happen.

Story sounds

The stories' own sounds (phase 2): played at moments of a story's timeline, and for explore mode's picks, results and actions. Each story sets its pitch and length (the sliders); in a headset each comes from where it happens in the scene, on a screen it is panned by where it shows.

Continuous sounds

Kept going while a story wants them (a robot vacuum while it moves, a machine while it runs), faded in and out; the pitch follows the story (a robot's speed).

A story's sounds

A story's whole sound track as a visitor hears it with the default volume (75 %): its cues at their times, its continuous sounds, its background, and its narration where it has a recording, the effects 9 dB under the voice while it plays. The cues are read from the app (it loads in a hidden frame, a few seconds). Rendering takes a moment.


  
Backgrounds

  
One quiet loop for the story on show, faded in once it is built and out when it goes. Every story without one of its own gets the calm tone, its pitch, brightness and pace from its key.

  

  
3D demo

  
A small sound circling your head at ear height, 1.5 m away, through the same HRTF panner a headset uses (the listener at your head). Use headphones: on speakers it is only left and right.

  
  
Ducking demo

  
A background and a few clicks; after two seconds seven seconds of the airports' narration play, and everything else dips 9 dB under the voice (down in about 0.4 s, back in about 1.5 s). Without the recording a stand-in voice plays.

  
  
Voice limit

  
At most 16 sounds at once: the one quietest now makes room for the next.
