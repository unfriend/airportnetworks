# The design room: contract

What `stories/design.js` is and what it promises. The engine is the flock's (`tools/flock/engine/`), adapted; its
shared-room, headset-setting and subtitle lines are the same as in every story.

## Not one of the stories

- Its entry is in `self.STORY_EXTRA` (`stories/list.js`), with the card fields of the others and `life: true`. The shell
  finds a key in either list when it starts, loads, swaps or titles a story (`HOST.meta`); everything that lists the
  stories (the Stories screens, Arrange all, `HOST.count`, Repeat's next story) sees only `STORY_LIST`; the station
  picker (the page's Add a station, the headset's Add here) lists it after the stories. `tools/release.mjs` lists it in `version.json`, so live updates swap it like a story.
- No narration and no chapters to sit through: one chapter (`table`, 6 s: the table fades in, the camera walks up to
  it, one line of text) that the room opens past (`APP.enterExplore()` at start unless `#design&t=` asks for it); a
  new wearer or Intro plays it. Its end opens explore mode, never Repeat (`HOST.ended()` is not called).

## The table (`sets/table.js`)

- Life size: 1 unit is 1 m, the floor at y = 0, the table round the origin, radius 1.15 m, its top at 0.86 m. In a
  headset the floor is 1.6 m under your eyes (`local` space) and the table's middle 1.55 m ahead (`xrPlace`), its near
  edge 40 cm in front of you; views never move it. In passthrough the floor is hidden (`table_floor` 0).
- The stories round the rim in `HOST.list`'s order, from the front (+z, your side) round to the right, each with a
  share of the rim of (its count + 1.2) parts. A plate (an annular sector) per story in its colour darkened, a bright
  edge in its colour and a place card at the rim (kicker and title, one canvas row each). Its miniatures stand on
  rings from the rim inwards (0.98, 0.82, 0.66, 0.5 m), 16 cm apart, 12 cm tall, turned to face out.
- The stage: a drum of radius 0.28 m in the middle; the picked prop stands on it 40 cm tall (a clone of its
  miniature), grows in over half a second and turns once in 14 s.
- The models: `feed(LOWPOLY_MODELS)` queues them; `update` builds for up to 5 ms a frame (at least one), the picked one
  first. `built()` and `total()` say how far it has got (the panes too). Each miniature has an unseen box round it
  (`pickables()`) for the pointer, sized to the model.
- Hover: the miniature grows 15 %, a cyan ring and a soft glow under it, its name over it. Picked: a ring in its
  story's colour under it.

## The cards

`DESIGN_CARDS` is generated by `build.mjs`, one per miniature, in the miniatures' order: `{ story, set, name, tris, id,
shows, size, frame, params: [[name, range, meaning, step]], views: [[name, what it frames]], reusedBy, reuses, file,
manifest, note }`. Only these fields of `props.json`, shortened (a meaning to its first clause, at most 110 characters;
a range 40). The hand-written stories' scenes (airports, proteins, spatial) have no set entry: their card has the
story's file and its scenes, no params or views, and says so. Two sets of one file (the crowd and the Sankey diagram,
`clinic.js`) say so; `micro.organs` says it is a library of models, not a scene. The page's card lists every param and
view; the headset's lists as many params as fit and says how many more.

## Shared rooms

- `state()`: `{ pick, '<miniature key>': [x, y, z, qx, qy, qz, qw, s], ... }`: the prop on the stage (`''` none) and one
  top-level field per miniature moved by hand (where it was put in the table's frame, mm; its turn, 3 decimals; its
  size, a factor on its own, 2 decimals), so two people moving two miniatures at once both keep theirs. A miniature
  without a field stands at home. Never the camera, the hover, the tab or anything per device.
- `apply(state())` gives the same state back: a record is kept exactly as given, and a miniature glides to it. Bad
  values (a wrong key, a record of the wrong length, outside 6 m, below the floor, a size outside half to four
  times) are ignored. Tidy clears every record (all go home, for everyone at the station).
- Effects: none. `view()` is null (life size). `place` and `panel` as the flock's: only the panel moves.
- Its station (the shell's, `shared.js`): placed by the headset menu's *Design room here* (at your feet, facing your
  way; the room's one moved there when it has one) or the page's Add a station, refused while the circles are locked;
  never by Arrange all, which leaves it where it is. Its clock starts in explore mode (at the table), and its icon over
  the ring is its own miniature, `design.table` (`icon` in its entry; skipped by `build.mjs`: not a prop). Outside a
  room the menu's *Design room* opens it (`HOST.go('design')`).

## In a headset

- The trigger or a pinch on a miniature puts it on the stage; pointing lights it (its name over it).
- The grip picks it up (`xrGrab`, after the shared line `if (XR.stories || HOST.anchor.locked(h.kind)) return;`, so
  not when anchored, away from its station or with its story locked): it keeps its offset from the hand and, with
  free moving on, turns with it (off: it stays upright and turns only about the vertical); the right stick pushes it
  away or pulls it in. Both grips on it size it (half to four times) and move it with their middle; when one lets go
  the other carries on. Let go, it stays where it was put (`drop`: its record).
- The panel (`drawExplorePane`): Prop (the card) and All props (the stories in three columns, then the chosen story's
  props), Tidy the table, Clear the stage. Every pane exists twice (HTML `renderPanes`, canvas `draw*Pane`).

## Tests

`node tools/smoke.mjs` (its design block): not on the Stories screen, all 45 miniatures built, a click on one putting
it on the stage with its card matching `props.json` (every card's params, views and file), a pretend headset's grip
carrying one and both grips sizing it, the state with it and back, a state from the room taken, bad values ignored,
Tidy, the headset panels, the intro ending at the table. How it feels in a headset is untested.
