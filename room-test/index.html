Room and depth test

This page finds out what a Meta Quest 3 tells a web page about the real room around you, and whether our app could use it: can a real hand or person hide virtual things, and can the room scan (walls, tables, sofas) hide them too?

It uses the same 3D engine as the app. In the headset a panel in front of you shows what came back; point at its buttons with a controller or a hand and pull the trigger (or pinch).

  • Depth picture: what the headset measures, as colours over the room (near warm, far cool).
  • Occlusion: test things (pillars at 1, 1.5, 2 and 3 m, a ball circling you, a big panel at 2.5 m); put a hand or a person in front of them: they should hide them.
  • Room: the room scan drawn, each part with its name.
  • Room hides: the room scan hides the test things behind real walls and furniture (the hand does not).
  • Nothing: the test things alone, no depth asked for, to compare the times with.

Stay a few seconds in each mode: each one's frame time, and how long the headset's graphics chip takes over a frame, are measured. If a hand hides things in the wrong places (beside it, or upside down), press Depth as on the panel to try another way of reading the depth, and note which one looked right; Draw with switches between the 3D engine and plain WebGL.

Checking for WebXR...

Try VR too records whether depth sensing is on in VR as well. A Quest may leave VR at once when a page asks for many features: if it does, that is an answer too.

Please send the report back: paste it into the chat, or Download it and send the file. Also take a photo in the headset (Meta button, then the trigger) of Occlusion and of Room hides with a hand in front of the test things.

What the report holds: your browser's version (from its user agent), what the headset gave (features, depth formats and sizes, a few depth values, the room scan's part names and sizes) and the frame times. No names, no pictures, no positions of the room. It is kept on this device (until the next test) and leaves it only when you copy, download or send it.

This page is a test, not part of the app. It has been checked in a desktop browser with a pretend headset only: it is untested on a real Quest.
